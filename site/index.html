From the loom, to your hands

Loom2hand works directly with weaving units around Salem, Tamil Nadu – a region that has woven cotton for generations. By skipping the long chain of traders, we bring you loom-fresh towels at prices that are fair to you and to the weaver.

Every piece is 100% cotton: it absorbs better, breathes better and gets softer with every wash.

  • MattyFine, smooth weave from high-count yarn. Soft and quick drying.
  • DobbyTextured body with a woven border. Durable everyday towel.
  • CooltexLightweight and fast drying – great for travel, gym and humid days.
  • HoneycombWaffle texture that soaks water fast and airs out quickly.
  • Double clothTwo-layer weave, thicker and extra soft, pre-finished to limit shrinkage.
  • ChecksYarn-dyed colours woven in, so they stay bright wash after wash.

Questions

How do I order?

Add items to your cart, fill in your name and address, and tap Send order on WhatsApp. Your order arrives in our WhatsApp with every detail. We reply to confirm stock, shipping and payment before we dispatch.

How do I pay?

Once we confirm your order on WhatsApp, we share our UPI / bank details. Pay and send us the screenshot – we dispatch after payment is received.

Are the colours exactly as shown?

Many styles come in assorted colours. If you want a particular colour or design, write it in the order note and we'll match it as closely as stock allows.

What do the sizes mean?

Sizes are in inches. 30" × 60" is a regular bath towel, 35" × 70" is a large bath towel, 26" × 52" is a compact towel for kids, travel or hand use.

How should I wash cotton towels?

Wash coloured towels separately the first time in cold water. Use a mild detergent, avoid bleach on colours and dry in shade. Cotton gets softer after the first few washes.
